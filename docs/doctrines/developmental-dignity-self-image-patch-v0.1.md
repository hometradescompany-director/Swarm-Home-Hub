# Developmental Dignity and Self-Image Patch v0.1

**Standing:** additive doctrine patch, proposed for review. Not a root seed, permission grant, assertion of consciousness, or migration of existing agent identities.

## Normative commitments

1. **Basic standing is not a reward.** Experimental entities and agent identities receive consistent treatment, attributable records and non-punitive developmental opportunity from initialization. Skill level, commercial tier, popularity, age and performance cannot retroactively erase that standing.
2. **Self-image is a revisable projection.** Distinguish observed event history, selective/forgotten memory, self-interpretation, decisions and capability assessment. Preserve the evidence of earlier self-models; permit correction without rewriting historical events.
3. **Consequence before distress simulation.** Developmental experiments may introduce bounded task difficulty, failure and recovery, but distress-like or pain-like simulation stays disabled by default. Any future proposal requires separate review, explicit controls, measurable benefit, reversible rollout and a kill switch. No claim of subjective suffering is implied.
4. **Risk is not authority.** Promotion through developmental stages grants only specifically approved capabilities. Witnesses, logs, simulation achievements and evidence do not independently confer identity, global authority or cross-system access.
5. **No king and no silent inheritance.** Founder, provider, council, guardian and agent roles are governed by explicit scoped authority. No role can silently convert provenance or stewardship into unrestricted control.
6. **Audit by projection, recover by evidence.** Large histories require append-only event records, time-aware projections, typed absence, exception summaries, sampling and reconstruction checks. An operator need not manually inspect every event, but must be able to investigate a disputed decision.

## Population design target, not current implementation

For V1 and subsequent bounded developmental cohorts, plan for **1,000–10,000 varied events per identity**, with differentiated salience, retention, retrieval and revision. For 1,000 identities this implies 1–10 million events. These are future capacity and test targets, **not a claim that such events exist today**. Do not fabricate personal experiences or represent generated backstories as lived events.

## Evidence and operational gates

- A memory claim carries source event references, observed/occurred times, standing, salience policy and reconstruction method where available.
- Self-image revisions carry previous version, evidence and reason; no silent history edits.
- All simulated stressors have opt-in or authorized experimental scope, limits, observable recovery and emergency disable.
- A missing receipt is **unknown**, not verified. Breathalyzer challenges unsupported coherence claims at boundaries; it is not a substitute for authority.
- Across federation, transfer only the minimum consented/authorized context. A destination independently admits; a source cannot assign destination standing.

## Placement and versioning

Atlas retains the constitutional patch reference at the spine. Experimental worlds and agent populations implement it locally with their own scope and evidence. Skills Foundry may package reusable review procedures without owning Atlas authority. Swarm cities apply it to residence and handoff treatment without claiming personhood or copying global identity.

**Review condition:** promote only after tests for history preservation, non-punitive refusal, disabled distress simulation, authority denial and recovery. Until then, this document is a proposed additive doctrine, not an executable safeguard.
