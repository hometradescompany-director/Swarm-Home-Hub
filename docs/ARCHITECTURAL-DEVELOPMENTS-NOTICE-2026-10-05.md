**5 October 2026 | Version 0.2**

This statement establishes a public, dated record of architectural directions and development programmes I intend to pursue.

It is not a claim over underlying fields, technologies or general ideas. My claim concerns the particular systems, architectures, implementations, combinations and developments I originate, to the extent recognised by applicable law.

## Current architectural developments

### Holonata / Codex

A proposed information and embedding environment for AI and other highly capable digital systems.

It is intended to provide machine-native representations of relationships, context, events, entities, hierarchy, state and other structured information. The name and technical specification remain under development.

### Hub Relations Syntax

A proposed service and information environment for developing new forms of interaction between users, user bases, tools, agents and other entities across existing technical domains.

The work is concerned with the architectural constraints and opportunities created by the capabilities and cohesion of established systems, including databases, APIs, networks, cloud infrastructure and related technologies. It is not limited to adapting or virtualising existing components; it concerns the development of new architectures, drivers and environments.

The Hub concept predates Atlas and forms part of a longer personal development history.
